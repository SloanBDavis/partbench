Status: **Completed (2026-09-01).**

Slices A–D landed. The named closer `pnpm smoke:v24-finished-parts` is

## Product goal

A person or the connected agent can finish one exact solid using the completed
V23 matrix plus three missing moves: (1) combine existing exact bodies (union
and subtract), (2) offset datum planes from world XY/XZ/YZ or a planar face,
and sketch/feature on those planes, (3) pattern a hole or other completed
face-attached feature linearly or circularly, not the whole body. Move/align a
body onto a planar face or datum is in scope if combine is otherwise useless
(two cylinders that do not share a plane).

After V24 this is true in `pnpm dev` and in `scenarios/` CADOps.

Proof parts, not a family dump:

- Stepped pulley: two coaxial cylinders fused, then a bore.
- Clevis: base plate, two ears, one hole through the ears.
- Flange: disc, center bore, circular hole pattern.

If a slice does not make one of those parts more finishable, delete it.

## Why this

V23 made current-exact faces and edges commandable. Finished parts still stop
at one body. You cannot fuse two solids, sketch on an offset plane that is not
a face, or pattern a hole around a disc. That is the bottleneck. One release.

## Binding rules

1. CADOps is the center. Human UI and the connected agent submit the same
   batch. Do not add an MCP tool unless `cad.batch` cannot express the ops.
2. Reuse V13 checkpoint/anchor plus V23 promotion. Datums are real source, not
   V22 display sections.
3. New source records are allowed. Prove a project-schema bump is required
   before adding one. `.wcad` stays `partbench.wcad.v2` unless a persist gap is
   recorded in this plan first.
4. Universal selection is not universal commandability.
5. Approval modes stay `manualApproval` and `approveAll`.
6. Vertices stay inspect-only.
7. Do not add a workspace package or production dependency unless this doc
   names it.

## Frozen matrix

Facts in the current protocol. Do not invent a second copy of these ops.

- `feature.extrude` add/cut booleans a profile into a target body. It does not
  combine two completed solids.
- `feature.linearPattern` and `feature.circularPattern` take `seedBodyId` only.
- `sketch.create` takes world `XY` / `XZ` / `YZ` only. `sketch.createOnFace`
  takes a planar face.
- `feature.mirror` can offset a standard plane for that one op. That is not a
  persistent datum.
- `scene.updateTransform` moves display primitives. It is not body align.

### Existing collectors that grow

| Collector | Today | V24 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sketch plane | `sketch.create` world XY/XZ/YZ | Also a persistent offset datum |
| Sketch-on-face | planar face via V23 promotion | Keep. Do not replace with datums. |
| Mirror plane | standard plane, generated/named/anchor face, optional one-shot offset | Also a persistent datum |
| Linear/circular pattern seed | `seedBodyId` whole body | Also a completed `feature.hole`. Body seed stays. |
| Hole target | body | Keep. Pattern instances remain holes on that body. |
| Extrude/revolve add/cut | profile into one target | Keep. Not a substitute for combine. |

Do not add `feature.linearFeaturePattern`. Grow the existing pattern ops.
The frozen patterned seed is `feature.hole`. Do not widen to every feature
kind unless a proof part cannot finish without it.

### New CADOps

| Op | Why it is missing | Consumed by |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `feature.combine` | No union/subtract of two completed exact solids | Pulley, clevis |
| `datum.plane.create` | No persistent offset plane source | Grown sketch plane and mirror plane |
| `feature.align` | Only if slice A cannot build the pulley without it | Combine of two cylinders that do not share a plane |

`feature.combine` mode is `union` or `subtract`. Both inputs are completed
exact solids. Generic boolean on non-solid junk stays deleted.

`datum.plane.create` offsets from world XY/XZ/YZ or from a planar face
(generated, named, or active-anchor, including V23 promotion). The datum is
document source. Sketch and feature on it through the grown sketch-plane
collector. Do not add a second sketch op unless `sketch.create` cannot carry
a datum id.
